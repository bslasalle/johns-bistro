We believe great food brings people together. Whether your out for a meal with friends or grabbing lunch, John's Bistro is the place to be.

Our Story

John O'Barra, early in his career as a chef

From Passion to Plate

John O'Barra grew up in a small fishing village in County Down, where food was always at the heart of family life. After training at La Salle Cookery School, John left with a simple dream: to open a restaurant that served honest, tasty food using the best local ingredients.

John's Bistro opened its doors in 2009 on the Lisburn Road. From day one, the idea has been simple — good food, good service, and a place where everyone feels welcome.

Today, the bistro is a well-known spot on the Lisburn Road and employs a team of fifteen people, many of whom have been with John from the start. It remains a family-run restaurant with strong ties to the local community.
